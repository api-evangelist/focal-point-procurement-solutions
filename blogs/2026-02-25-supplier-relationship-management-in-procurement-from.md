For years, Supplier Relationship Management in procurement was largely a compliance exercise. Teams were tracking contracts, collecting documentation, verifying insurance and running the same risk mitigation playbook with each supplier. Compliance was the name of the game, and little to strategic leverage was being generated within supplier relationships.
